# Asim Munir and POWERUS Delegation Discuss Defence Technology

By Syeda Manal Tirmizi

RAWALPINDI, September 16, 2026: Chief of Army Staff and Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir has met a delegation from US-based POWERUS at General Headquarters to discuss defence technology, procurement, manufacturing and critical-infrastructure capabilities.

The delegation was led by Robert Bret Velicovich, according to reports citing the Inter-Services Public Relations. The talks covered technological innovation and possible cooperation in areas of mutual interest.

[Aaj News](https://english.aaj.tv/news/330472663/field-marshal-stresses-tech-innovation-for-national-security) described POWERUS as a company specializing in defence and critical-infrastructure solutions. The meeting examined how technical expertise and industrial capacity could support modern security requirements.

## Technology and domestic capacity

Munir emphasized the role of technological innovation in strengthening national-security capabilities and preparing institutions for emerging threats. Rapid changes in surveillance, communications, unmanned systems and infrastructure protection have made technology a central element of defence planning and procurement.

The discussion also covered defence production and manufacturing. Building sustainable capacity inside Pakistan could reduce long-term dependence on imported systems while allowing local institutions to adapt equipment to their operational needs.

No contracts, procurement values or specific systems were announced after the meeting. The talks were presented as an exploration of potential areas for future engagement rather than a completed acquisition.

That distinction matters because defence-industry meetings can range from preliminary technical briefings to formal negotiations. Any eventual procurement or production arrangement would require further institutional review and official approval.

## Wider Pakistan-US engagement

The meeting took place against a backdrop of continuing Pakistan-US contact on security and regional developments. Defence ties have remained one part of that relationship, alongside counterterrorism, economic engagement and diplomatic discussions.

Participants also exchanged views on the regional situation, according to [Dunya News](https://dunyanews.tv/en/Pakistan/973230-field-marshal-asim-munir-us-delegation-discuss-defence-technology-and).

Both sides indicated that engagement would continue as they assess areas where technology, manufacturing and specialized expertise could support defence and critical-infrastructure needs. The next steps, however, will depend on whether those broad discussions produce defined projects or formal agreements.
