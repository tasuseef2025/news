# SEO And Indexing Audit - 2026-08-26

## Scope

This audit reviewed the Next.js routes, MongoDB publishing state, article eligibility rules, metadata, structured data, author/category archives, robots directives, XML sitemap, Google News sitemap, RSS feed, and representative public URLs. Audit and validation scripts are read-only and do not rewrite MongoDB.

## Baseline Findings

- MongoDB contained 2,379 records with `status=published` at audit time.
- Review state was inconsistent: 991 were approved, 114 needed review, and 1,274 had no review status.
- The content audit classified 314 records as `KEEP`, 119 as `IMPROVE`, 1,918 as `REVIEW`, and 28 as `CONSOLIDATE`. It did not recommend automatic removal.
- Fifty-nine records contained likely automation or prompt leakage and should remain out of discovery until edited.
- Twenty-eight records had high duplicate risk and need a human canonical/merge decision.
- Live `/sitemap.xml` contained 1,021 unique HTTPS URLs with no duplicate or foreign-host URLs.
- Live `/news-sitemap.xml` contained 15 recent URLs and passed the 48-hour publication-date checks.
- A sampled set of 25 sitemap URLs produced no redirect, 404, `noindex`, or canonical findings.
- The freshly built local sitemap contains 882 URLs after applying the stricter shared eligibility check; the News sitemap remains at 15. The production count will change only after deployment and will continue to change with editorial approvals.

Counts are a point-in-time snapshot and will change as stories are published or reviewed.

## Technical Corrections

- Public article eligibility now uses one shared quality decision across pages, sitemap, News sitemap, and RSS.
- Concise reporting is no longer rejected only because it is below 500 words. Extremely thin or incomplete records remain blocked.
- Prompt leakage, placeholders, malformed headings, raw model JSON, repeated filler, broken markup, and unsafe external-publisher bylines are detected.
- View increments and trending recalculation no longer rewrite editorial modification dates.
- Due scheduled stories become approved when they are automatically published.
- Empty and one-story category archives use `noindex,follow` and are excluded from the sitemap.
- Author pages now expose a `Person` entity and a visible list of eligible articles.
- RSS now includes an Atom self-link, language, build date, and permalink GUID values.
- The homepage has one semantic H1 while preserving the existing visual design.

## Editorial Work Still Required

- Review the 1,918 `REVIEW` rows in `exports/seo-audit-2026-08-26.csv`; most are legacy records missing an explicit review decision.
- Edit or quarantine the 59 prompt-leakage records.
- Resolve the 28 duplicate-risk records by choosing a canonical article, merging useful original reporting, and redirecting the retired URL.
- Replace publisher names used as Novexa bylines with a real Novexa author or `Novexa News Desk`. Preserve the external publisher only as source attribution.
- Do not bulk-publish drafts or bulk-delete articles based on word count.

## Deployment And Search Console

1. Deploy the code and confirm `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/news-sitemap.xml`, and `/rss.xml` return HTTP 200.
2. Re-run the sitemap validator against production.
3. Submit only the canonical sitemap URLs in Google Search Console.
4. Use URL Inspection on a small set of important, approved stories and request indexing after verifying rendered content and canonical selection.
5. Monitor indexing and crawl reports for several weeks. Sitemap inclusion is a discovery signal, not an indexing guarantee.

The implementation follows Google's guidance that normal sitemaps use canonical absolute URLs and accurate modification dates, while News sitemaps contain only articles published during the previous two days.
